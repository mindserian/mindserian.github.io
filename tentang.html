Tentang kami

Laman Minda disediakan oleh Unit Psikiatri dan Kesihatan Mental, Hospital Serian, untuk pesakit dan keluarga.

Perkhidmatan kami

  • Klinik pakar psikiatri (pesakit luar)
  • Lawatan rumah oleh Jururawat Psikiatri Komuniti (CPN)
  • Suntikan depot dan susulan ubat
  • Rujukan dari wad dan Jabatan Kecemasan

Temu janji

Untuk mendapatkan, menukar atau bertanya tentang temu janji, hubungi talian unit semasa waktu pejabat.

Apa yang perlu dibawa

  • Buku temu janji dan kad pengenalan
  • Semua ubat yang anda makan sekarang
  • Pelan Keselamatan atau Pelan Cegah Relaps anda, jika ada
  • Soalan yang anda mahu tanya
  • Ahli keluarga atau penjaga, jika boleh

Kandungan laman ini

Setiap modul ditulis oleh kakitangan unit berdasarkan garis panduan KKM, dan disemak oleh Pakar Psikiatri sebelum diterbitkan. Kandungan disemak semula setiap 12 bulan. Jika anda jumpa maklumat yang salah, sila beritahu kami.

About us

Laman Minda is made by the Psychiatry and Mental Health Unit, Hospital Serian, for patients and their families.

Our services

  • Psychiatry specialist clinic (outpatients)
  • Home visits by the Community Psychiatric Nurse (CPN)
  • Depot injections and medicine follow-up
  • Referrals from the wards and Emergency Department

Appointments

To get, change or ask about an appointment, call the unit line during office hours.

What to bring

  • Your appointment book and IC
  • All the medicines you take now
  • Your Safety Plan or Relapse Plan, if you have one
  • Questions you want to ask
  • A family member or carer, if possible

About this content

Every module is written by unit staff based on Ministry of Health guidelines, and reviewed by a Consultant Psychiatrist before publishing. Content is reviewed every 12 months. If you find something wrong, please tell us.
