Rutin bertenang yang mudah

  1. Malapkan lampu.
  2. Simpan skrin.
  3. Lakukan sesuatu yang tenang: mandi air suam, muzik lembut, berdoa atau membaca.
  4. Tulis senarai tugas esok supaya minda boleh berehat.
  5. Lakukan beberapa minit pernafasan perlahan atau imbasan badan di atas katil.

Bekerja syif?

Kekalkan waktu tidur setetap mungkin, halang cahaya matahari dengan langsir gelap, dan lindungi waktu tidur anda daripada gangguan.

A simple wind-down routine

  1. Dim the lights.
  2. Put screens away.
  3. Do something calm: a warm shower, gentle music, prayer or reading.
  4. Write tomorrow's to-do list so your mind can let go.
  5. Do a few minutes of slow breathing or a body scan in bed.

Working shifts?

Keep your sleep time as regular as you can, block out daylight with dark curtains, and protect your sleep time from interruptions.
