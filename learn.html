Getting startedPermulaan

What mental health is, and everyday ways to look after it.Apa itu kesihatan mental, dan cara harian menjaganya.

Mental health basicsAsas kesihatan mentalWhat mental health is, and how to look after itApa itu kesihatan mental, dan cara menjaganya

What is it?

Mental health is about how we think, feel and cope with daily life. Everyone has mental health, just as everyone has physical health, and it can change over time.

A mental health condition is when changes in thinking, mood or behaviour last for a while and make daily life hard. Conditions such as depression, anxiety and psychosis are common, and they are treatable.

Common signs

  • Changes in sleep or appetite, feeling tired all the time
  • Feeling low, worried or irritable most days
  • Finding it hard to concentrate, or thoughts that won't stop
  • Pulling away from family and friends
  • Using more alcohol, cigarettes or drugs to cope

What can I do to help myself?

  • Connect: spend time with people who make you feel supported.
  • Move: walk, garden or stretch. Any movement helps mood.
  • Rest: keep regular sleep and wake times. See Sleep.
  • Calm: learn one calming skill, such as slow breathing.
  • Meaning: do something that matters to you, such as faith, family, helping others or a hobby.

When should I seek help?

If changes last more than two weeks, make daily life hard, or you or others are worried about you. If you are thinking of ending your life, get urgent help.

Apakah ia?

Kesihatan mental ialah tentang cara kita berfikir, berasa dan menghadapi kehidupan harian. Setiap orang mempunyai kesihatan mental, sama seperti kesihatan fizikal, dan ia boleh berubah dari semasa ke semasa.

Masalah kesihatan mental berlaku apabila perubahan dalam pemikiran, mood atau tingkah laku berlarutan dan menyukarkan kehidupan harian. Masalah seperti kemurungan, kebimbangan dan psikosis adalah biasa, dan boleh dirawat.

Tanda-tanda biasa

  • Perubahan tidur atau selera makan, sentiasa rasa letih
  • Rasa sedih, risau atau mudah marah hampir setiap hari
  • Sukar menumpukan perhatian, atau fikiran yang tidak berhenti
  • Menjauhkan diri daripada keluarga dan kawan
  • Lebih banyak menggunakan alkohol, rokok atau dadah untuk menghadapi masalah

Apa yang boleh saya lakukan untuk membantu diri?

  • Berhubung: luangkan masa dengan orang yang menyokong anda.
  • Bergerak: berjalan, berkebun atau meregangkan badan. Sebarang pergerakan membantu mood.
  • Berehat: kekalkan waktu tidur dan bangun yang tetap. Lihat Tidur.
  • Bertenang: pelajari satu kemahiran menenangkan diri, seperti pernafasan perlahan.
  • Bermakna: lakukan sesuatu yang penting bagi anda, seperti agama, keluarga, membantu orang lain atau hobi.

Bila perlu mendapatkan bantuan?

Jika perubahan berlarutan lebih daripada dua minggu, menyukarkan kehidupan harian, atau anda atau orang lain bimbang tentang anda. Jika anda terfikir untuk menamatkan nyawa, dapatkan bantuan segera.

Myth or fact?Mitos atau fakta?Common beliefs about mental health, checkedKepercayaan biasa tentang kesihatan mental, disemak

“Mental health problems are a sign of weakness.”

Myth Mental health conditions can affect anyone, whatever their strength, faith, education or background. They are health conditions, not a failure of character.

“Seeking help means you are weak.”

Myth Getting support is a way of looking after your health, just like seeing a doctor for diabetes or high blood pressure.

“Most people with a mental health condition are violent.”

Myth Most people with mental health conditions are never violent. They are more likely to be harmed by others than to harm anyone.

“Asking someone about suicide puts the idea in their head.”

Myth Asking directly and calmly does not increase risk. It often brings relief, and it opens the door to getting help.

“All psychiatric medicines are addictive.”

Myth Most, such as antidepressants and antipsychotics, are not addictive. Some sleeping or calming pills can cause dependence, which is why doctors use them carefully and for a short time.

“Many people with mental health conditions recover and live full lives.”

Fact With the right treatment and support, many people recover or manage their condition well. Starting treatment early helps.

“Masalah kesihatan mental ialah tanda kelemahan.”

Mitos Masalah kesihatan mental boleh berlaku kepada sesiapa sahaja, tanpa mengira kekuatan, iman, pendidikan atau latar belakang. Ia keadaan kesihatan, bukan kelemahan peribadi.

“Mendapatkan bantuan bermakna anda lemah.”

Mitos Mendapatkan sokongan ialah satu cara menjaga kesihatan anda, sama seperti berjumpa doktor untuk kencing manis atau darah tinggi.

“Kebanyakan orang yang mempunyai masalah kesihatan mental bersikap ganas.”

Mitos Kebanyakan mereka tidak pernah ganas. Mereka lebih berkemungkinan menjadi mangsa berbanding mencederakan orang lain.

“Bertanya tentang bunuh diri akan menanam idea itu dalam fikiran seseorang.”

Mitos Bertanya secara terus dan tenang tidak meningkatkan risiko. Ia selalunya melegakan, dan membuka jalan untuk mendapatkan bantuan.

“Semua ubat psikiatri menyebabkan ketagihan.”

Mitos Kebanyakan ubat, seperti antidepresan dan antipsikotik, tidak menyebabkan ketagihan. Sesetengah ubat tidur atau penenang boleh menyebabkan kebergantungan, sebab itu doktor menggunakannya dengan berhati-hati dan untuk tempoh singkat.

“Ramai orang yang mempunyai masalah kesihatan mental pulih dan menjalani kehidupan yang bermakna.”

Fakta Dengan rawatan dan sokongan yang betul, ramai yang pulih atau dapat mengurus keadaan mereka dengan baik. Rawatan awal membantu.

AnxietyKebimbangan

When worry, fear or panic feel too strong or last too long.Apabila risau, takut atau panik terasa terlalu kuat atau berpanjangan.

Anxiety and panicKebimbangan dan panikWorry, tension, panic attacks and a body on alertRisau, tegang, serangan panik dan badan yang berjaga-jaga

What is it?

Anxiety is a feeling of worry, fear or unease. It is the body's natural alarm system, and everyone feels it sometimes. It becomes a problem when it is strong, happens often, or goes on when there is no real danger.

A panic attack is a sudden, strong wave of fear with strong body feelings. It is frightening, but on its own it is not dangerous. It usually peaks within about 10 minutes, then eases.

Common signs

  • Fast heartbeat, fast breathing, sweating, shaking or dizziness
  • Feeling nervous, on edge or restless
  • Worry that is hard to stop, expecting the worst
  • Avoiding places or situations, seeking reassurance a lot

Don't assume it is "just anxiety"

Call 999 or go to the Emergency Department if it is the first time you have these symptoms, if chest pain spreads to your arm, jaw, neck or back, comes with sweating or vomiting, or if you faint or are very breathless. Feeling anxious does not rule out a heart problem. If in doubt, get checked.

How is it treated?

Talking therapy, learning coping skills and, for some people, medicine. Ask your treatment team what suits you.

What can I do to help myself?

  • Slow your breathing, with a longer breath out: slow breathing.
  • Ground yourself in the present: 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
  • During panic, tell yourself: "This is anxiety. It is uncomfortable, but it will pass."
  • Face fears step by step. Avoiding them makes anxiety grow.
  • Cut down on caffeine, and avoid alcohol.

When should I seek help?

If anxiety lasts most days for a few weeks, or stops you doing everyday things.

Apakah ia?

Kebimbangan ialah perasaan risau, takut atau tidak tenteram. Ia sistem penggera semula jadi badan, dan setiap orang mengalaminya sekali-sekala. Ia menjadi masalah apabila terlalu kuat, kerap berlaku, atau berterusan walaupun tiada bahaya sebenar.

Serangan panik ialah gelombang ketakutan yang kuat dan mendadak, disertai perasaan fizikal yang kuat. Ia menakutkan, tetapi dengan sendirinya ia tidak berbahaya. Biasanya ia memuncak dalam kira-kira 10 minit, kemudian reda.

Tanda-tanda biasa

  • Jantung berdebar laju, nafas laju, berpeluh, menggeletar atau pening
  • Rasa gementar, resah atau tidak senang duduk
  • Risau yang sukar dihentikan, menjangkakan perkara paling buruk
  • Mengelak tempat atau situasi tertentu, kerap meminta kepastian

Jangan anggap ia "hanya kebimbangan"

Hubungi 999 atau pergi ke Jabatan Kecemasan jika ini kali pertama anda mengalami simptom ini, jika sakit dada merebak ke lengan, rahang, leher atau belakang, disertai peluh atau muntah, atau jika anda pengsan atau sangat sesak nafas. Rasa cemas tidak bermakna tiada masalah jantung. Jika ragu-ragu, dapatkan pemeriksaan.

Bagaimana ia dirawat?

Terapi bercakap, mempelajari kemahiran menghadapi masalah dan, bagi sesetengah orang, ubat. Tanya pasukan rawatan anda apa yang sesuai.

Apa yang boleh saya lakukan untuk membantu diri?

  • Perlahankan pernafasan, dengan hembusan yang lebih panjang: pernafasan perlahan.
  • Kembali ke masa kini: latihan 5-4-3-2-1.
  • Semasa panik, katakan kepada diri: "Ini kebimbangan. Ia tidak selesa, tetapi ia akan berlalu."
  • Hadapi ketakutan langkah demi langkah. Mengelak menjadikan kebimbangan bertambah.
  • Kurangkan kafein, dan elakkan alkohol.

Bila perlu mendapatkan bantuan?

Jika kebimbangan berlaku hampir setiap hari selama beberapa minggu, atau menghalang anda melakukan perkara harian.

MoodMood

When mood is too low, or swings between high and low.Apabila mood terlalu rendah, atau berubah antara tinggi dan rendah.

Low mood and depressionMood rendah dan kemurunganMore than feeling sadLebih daripada sekadar rasa sedih

What is it?

Everyone feels low sometimes. Depression is when low mood, or loss of interest and enjoyment, lasts most of the day, nearly every day, for at least two weeks, and affects daily life. It is a common health condition, not a weakness, and it is treatable.

Common signs

  • Feeling sad, empty or hopeless
  • Losing interest in things you used to enjoy
  • Sleeping much more or much less, changes in appetite
  • Low energy, aches and pains, feeling slowed down
  • Feeling worthless or guilty, finding it hard to concentrate
  • Thoughts that life is not worth living

How is it treated?

Talking therapies, medicine, and support from family and community help most people recover. Antidepressants take time: the full effect can take up to 6 weeks, so don't stop early. Talk to your doctor first.

What can I do to help myself?

  • Do small things, even when you don't feel like it. Action often comes before motivation.
  • Keep a routine for sleep, meals and daylight.
  • Walk every day, starting with 10 minutes.
  • Tell one trusted person how you feel.
  • Avoid alcohol and drugs. They make low mood worse.

When should I seek help?

If low mood lasts more than two weeks or makes daily life hard. If you are thinking about ending your life, get urgent help now.

Apakah ia?

Setiap orang pernah rasa sedih. Kemurungan berlaku apabila mood rendah, atau hilang minat dan keseronokan, berlarutan sepanjang hari, hampir setiap hari, sekurang-kurangnya dua minggu, dan menjejaskan kehidupan harian. Ia masalah kesihatan yang biasa, bukan tanda kelemahan, dan boleh dirawat.

Tanda-tanda biasa

  • Rasa sedih, kosong atau putus asa
  • Hilang minat terhadap perkara yang dahulu digemari
  • Tidur terlalu banyak atau terlalu sedikit, perubahan selera makan
  • Kurang tenaga, sakit-sakit badan, rasa lembap
  • Rasa tidak berguna atau bersalah, sukar menumpukan perhatian
  • Fikiran bahawa hidup tidak bermakna

Bagaimana ia dirawat?

Terapi bercakap, ubat, dan sokongan keluarga serta komuniti membantu kebanyakan orang pulih. Ubat antidepresan mengambil masa: kesan penuhnya boleh mengambil sehingga 6 minggu, jadi jangan berhenti awal. Bincang dengan doktor anda dahulu.

Apa yang boleh saya lakukan untuk membantu diri?

  • Lakukan perkara kecil, walaupun anda tidak rasa mahu. Tindakan selalunya datang sebelum semangat.
  • Kekalkan rutin untuk tidur, makan dan cahaya matahari.
  • Berjalan setiap hari, bermula dengan 10 minit.
  • Beritahu seorang yang anda percayai tentang perasaan anda.
  • Elakkan alkohol dan dadah. Ia memburukkan mood.

Bila perlu mendapatkan bantuan?

Jika mood rendah berlarutan lebih daripada dua minggu atau menyukarkan kehidupan harian. Jika anda terfikir untuk menamatkan nyawa, dapatkan bantuan segera sekarang.

Bipolar disorderKecelaruan bipolarHighs and lows of mood, and keeping steadyMood tinggi dan rendah, dan cara kekal stabil

What is it?

Bipolar disorder is a long-term mood condition. People have periods of very high mood (mania or hypomania) and periods of low mood (depression), with stable periods in between. It cannot be cured, but with treatment most people stay well for long periods and live full lives.

Common signs

During a high (mania):

  • Feeling very happy, excited or irritable
  • Needing much less sleep but still full of energy
  • Racing thoughts, talking fast, jumping between ideas
  • Feeling overconfident, making big plans
  • Spending a lot of money, or risky, out-of-character behaviour
  • In severe episodes: hearing voices or holding unusual beliefs

During a low (depression): sadness, hopelessness, low energy, losing interest, sleep and appetite changes, and sometimes thoughts of suicide. See Depression.

What may trigger an episode?

  • Stress, and being very busy
  • Poor or irregular sleep
  • Alcohol and drugs (such as syabu or ganja)
  • Stopping medicine

Bipolar disorder often runs in families.

How is it treated?

  • Mood stabilisers and/or antipsychotic medicine, usually long term, to treat episodes and prevent new ones. Antidepressants are not used on their own.
  • Some people have a long-acting injection if taking tablets every day is difficult.
  • Talking therapy and education for you and your family help prevent relapse.
  • If you take lithium, you will need regular blood tests. Drink enough fluids, especially in hot weather or when you are unwell, and check with the clinic before taking painkillers such as ibuprofen. Vomiting, diarrhoea, a worsening shake, unsteadiness or confusion can mean too much lithium: see My medicine.

Pregnancy

Some bipolar medicines (for example sodium valproate) are not safe in pregnancy. If you are pregnant or planning a pregnancy, talk to your doctor early. If you are a woman or girl who could become pregnant, use reliable contraception while taking valproate; your doctor will review this with you at least once a year. If you think you might be pregnant, contact the clinic straight away. Don't stop your medicine on your own.

What can I do to help myself?

  • Keep a regular routine, especially sleep and wake times.
  • Learn your own early warning signs, such as sleeping less, talking faster, spending more, or withdrawing. Share them with family.
  • Keep a simple mood diary.
  • Avoid alcohol and drugs, and limit caffeine.
  • Agree a plan with family for what to do if early signs appear.

When should I seek help?

Contact your treatment team early if warning signs appear. Get urgent help if someone has not slept for days, is behaving in a risky or unsafe way, is very confused, or has thoughts of suicide: Get Help.

Apakah ia?

Kecelaruan bipolar ialah masalah mood jangka panjang. Penghidapnya mengalami tempoh mood sangat tinggi (mania atau hipomania) dan tempoh mood rendah (kemurungan), dengan tempoh stabil di antaranya. Ia tidak boleh disembuhkan sepenuhnya, tetapi dengan rawatan kebanyakan orang kekal sihat untuk tempoh yang lama dan menjalani kehidupan yang bermakna.

Tanda-tanda biasa

Semasa mood tinggi (mania):

  • Rasa terlalu gembira, teruja atau mudah marah
  • Perlu tidur jauh lebih sedikit tetapi masih penuh tenaga
  • Fikiran berlumba-lumba, bercakap laju, melompat dari satu idea ke idea lain
  • Terlalu yakin diri, membuat rancangan besar
  • Berbelanja banyak, atau tingkah laku berisiko yang luar biasa
  • Dalam episod teruk: mendengar suara atau mempunyai kepercayaan yang luar biasa

Semasa mood rendah (kemurungan): sedih, putus asa, kurang tenaga, hilang minat, perubahan tidur dan selera makan, dan kadangkala fikiran untuk membunuh diri. Lihat Kemurungan.

Apa yang boleh mencetuskan episod?

  • Tekanan, dan terlalu sibuk
  • Tidur yang tidak cukup atau tidak tetap
  • Alkohol dan dadah (seperti syabu atau ganja)
  • Berhenti makan ubat

Kecelaruan bipolar selalunya berlaku dalam keluarga.

Bagaimana ia dirawat?

  • Ubat penstabil mood dan/atau ubat antipsikotik, biasanya jangka panjang, untuk merawat episod dan mencegah episod baharu. Antidepresan tidak digunakan secara bersendirian.
  • Sesetengah orang menerima suntikan jangka panjang jika sukar mengambil pil setiap hari.
  • Terapi bercakap dan pendidikan untuk anda dan keluarga membantu mencegah relaps.
  • Jika anda mengambil lithium, anda perlu menjalani ujian darah secara berkala. Minum air secukupnya, terutama semasa cuaca panas atau ketika sakit, dan tanya klinik dahulu sebelum mengambil ubat tahan sakit seperti ibuprofen. Muntah, cirit-birit, tangan menggeletar yang semakin teruk, rasa tidak stabil atau keliru mungkin tanda litium terlalu tinggi: lihat Ubat saya.

Kehamilan

Sesetengah ubat bipolar (contohnya sodium valproate) tidak selamat semasa hamil. Jika anda hamil atau merancang untuk hamil, berbincang dengan doktor anda lebih awal. Jika anda wanita atau remaja perempuan yang boleh hamil, gunakan kaedah pencegahan kehamilan yang berkesan semasa mengambil valproate; doktor akan menyemaknya bersama anda sekurang-kurangnya setahun sekali. Jika anda rasa mungkin hamil, hubungi klinik dengan segera. Jangan hentikan ubat sendiri.

Apa yang boleh saya lakukan untuk membantu diri?

  • Kekalkan rutin yang tetap, terutamanya waktu tidur dan bangun.
  • Kenal pasti tanda amaran awal anda sendiri, seperti kurang tidur, bercakap lebih laju, berbelanja lebih, atau menyendiri. Kongsikan dengan keluarga.
  • Simpan diari mood yang ringkas.
  • Elakkan alkohol dan dadah, dan hadkan kafein.
  • Buat pelan bersama keluarga tentang apa yang perlu dibuat jika tanda awal muncul.

Bila perlu mendapatkan bantuan?

Hubungi pasukan rawatan anda lebih awal jika tanda amaran muncul. Dapatkan bantuan segera jika seseorang tidak tidur selama beberapa hari, berkelakuan berisiko atau tidak selamat, sangat keliru, atau mempunyai fikiran untuk membunuh diri: Dapatkan Bantuan.

PsychosisPsikosis

When it is hard to tell what is real.Apabila sukar membezakan apa yang nyata.

Psychosis and schizophreniaPsikosis dan skizofreniaHallucinations, delusions, and long-term careHalusinasi, delusi, dan rawatan jangka panjang

What is it?

Psychosis is when a person's sense of what is real is disturbed. They may:

  • hear or see things that others can't (hallucinations), most often hearing voices
  • hold strong beliefs that others don't share, such as being followed, watched or plotted against (delusions)
  • have muddled thinking or speech that is hard to follow

Psychosis can be caused by schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, severe depression, severe stress, drugs (such as syabu or ganja) or alcohol, and some physical illnesses.

Schizophrenia is a long-term condition where psychosis can come and go. It does not mean a "split personality", and most people with schizophrenia are not violent.

Other common signs

  • Withdrawing from family and friends
  • Losing interest and motivation, feeling "flat"
  • Neglecting washing, eating or daily tasks
  • Poor sleep, suspicion or fearfulness

How is it treated?

  • Antipsychotic medicine, as tablets or as an injection every few weeks. After a first episode, medicine is usually continued for at least 1 to 2 years, and longer for many people.
  • Stopping and starting medicine leads to more relapses and hospital stays. Never stop suddenly; plan any change with your team.
  • Family education about the illness, medicine and warning signs reduces relapse.
  • Talking therapy (CBT), and help with work and daily life.
  • Physical health checks: some medicines can cause weight gain, diabetes or high cholesterol, so weight, blood sugar, cholesterol and blood pressure are checked regularly. Tell your team about any side effects.

Earlier treatment works better. Many people wait a long time before getting treatment for psychosis. If you notice signs in yourself or someone you love, see a doctor early.

Early warning signs of relapse

These often appear days or weeks before a relapse. Learning your own signs, with your family, helps prevent it:

  • Sleeping badly or at odd times
  • Feeling tense, irritable or suspicious
  • Withdrawing, staying in your room
  • Trouble concentrating
  • Not looking after yourself as usual

What can I do to help myself?

  • Take your medicine regularly, and keep your appointments.
  • Avoid drugs (especially syabu and ganja) and alcohol. They can trigger relapse.
  • Keep a daily routine with regular sleep, meals and activity.
  • If you smoke, ask us for help to stop.

For family

  • Stay calm and speak simply. Don't argue about unusual beliefs; listen to the feelings behind them.
  • Help with medicine and appointments without forcing or blaming.
  • Look after yourself too. Ask our team about family education sessions.

When should I seek help?

Get urgent help if someone hears voices telling them to hurt themselves or others, is very confused or agitated, stops eating or drinking, or may not be safe: call 999 or go to the Emergency Department. See Get Help.

Apakah ia?

Psikosis berlaku apabila keupayaan seseorang untuk membezakan apa yang nyata terganggu. Dia mungkin:

  • mendengar atau melihat sesuatu yang orang lain tidak dengar atau lihat (halusinasi), paling kerap mendengar suara
  • mempunyai kepercayaan kuat yang tidak dikongsi oleh orang lain, seperti diekori, diperhati atau dianiaya (delusi)
  • mempunyai fikiran atau percakapan yang bercelaru dan sukar difahami

Psikosis boleh disebabkan oleh skizofrenia, kecelaruan bipolar, kemurungan yang teruk, tekanan yang teruk, dadah (seperti syabu atau ganja) atau alkohol, dan sesetengah penyakit fizikal.

Skizofrenia ialah masalah jangka panjang yang menyebabkan psikosis boleh datang dan pergi. Ia bukan bermaksud "personaliti berpecah", dan kebanyakan penghidap skizofrenia tidak ganas.

Tanda-tanda lain yang biasa

  • Menjauhkan diri daripada keluarga dan kawan
  • Hilang minat dan semangat, rasa "kosong"
  • Mengabaikan mandi, makan atau tugas harian
  • Tidur terganggu, curiga atau ketakutan

Bagaimana ia dirawat?

  • Ubat antipsikotik, dalam bentuk pil atau suntikan setiap beberapa minggu. Selepas episod pertama, ubat biasanya diteruskan sekurang-kurangnya 1 hingga 2 tahun, dan lebih lama bagi ramai orang.
  • Berhenti dan mula semula ubat menyebabkan lebih banyak relaps dan kemasukan ke hospital. Jangan berhenti secara mengejut; rancang sebarang perubahan bersama pasukan anda.
  • Pendidikan keluarga tentang penyakit, ubat dan tanda amaran mengurangkan relaps.
  • Terapi bercakap (CBT), dan bantuan untuk kerja dan kehidupan harian.
  • Pemeriksaan kesihatan fizikal: sesetengah ubat boleh menyebabkan kenaikan berat badan, kencing manis atau kolesterol tinggi, jadi berat badan, gula darah, kolesterol dan tekanan darah diperiksa secara berkala. Beritahu pasukan anda tentang sebarang kesan sampingan.

Rawatan awal lebih berkesan. Ramai orang lambat mendapatkan rawatan untuk psikosis. Jika anda perasan tanda-tanda pada diri sendiri atau orang tersayang, jumpa doktor lebih awal.

Tanda amaran awal relaps

Tanda ini selalunya muncul beberapa hari atau minggu sebelum relaps. Mengenal pasti tanda anda sendiri, bersama keluarga, membantu mencegahnya:

  • Tidur tidak lena atau pada waktu yang tidak menentu
  • Rasa tegang, mudah marah atau curiga
  • Menyendiri, berkurung dalam bilik
  • Sukar menumpukan perhatian
  • Tidak menjaga diri seperti biasa

Long-standing patterns in emotions, thinking and relationships.Corak jangka panjang dalam emosi, pemikiran dan hubungan.

Personality disordersKecelaruan personalitiWhat they are, the main types, and treatmentApa itu, jenis-jenis utama, dan rawatan

What is it?

Our personality is the way we usually think, feel, behave and relate to others. A personality disorder is when some of these patterns are long-standing (usually from the teenage years), show up in many areas of life, and cause a lot of distress or problems with relationships and coping.

About 1 in 20 people live with a personality disorder. It is not a character flaw or something a person chooses. It usually comes from a mix of genes and life experiences, which for many people include trauma or neglect in childhood.

Types

Doctors have often grouped personality disorders into three clusters:

Group A: odd or eccentricParanoid (very suspicious of others), schizoid (prefers to be alone), schizotypal (unusual thoughts and behaviour)
Group B: emotional and impulsiveBorderline, antisocial (disregard for others and rules), histrionic (strong need for attention), narcissistic (strong need for admiration)
Group C: anxious and fearfulAvoidant (fear of rejection), dependent (relies heavily on others), obsessive-compulsive or anankastic (perfectionism and control)

Newer guidelines (ICD-11) describe personality disorder mainly by how mild or severe it is, rather than by type.

How is it diagnosed?

A psychiatrist makes the diagnosis after careful assessment, often over more than one visit, because the signs can overlap with other conditions.

How is it treated?

  • Talking therapy is the main treatment, often for a year or longer. Examples include dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT) and mentalisation-based therapy (MBT).
  • There is no medicine that treats a personality disorder itself, but medicine can help with problems that come with it, such as depression or anxiety.
  • Many people improve a great deal over time, especially with support.

What can I do to help myself?

  • Learn about your condition and notice your triggers.
  • Keep a steady routine, with regular sleep.
  • Avoid alcohol and drugs.
  • Make a crisis plan with your team: who to call and what helps.

Apakah ia?

Personaliti ialah cara kita biasanya berfikir, berasa, berkelakuan dan berhubung dengan orang lain. Kecelaruan personaliti berlaku apabila sebahagian corak ini berlarutan lama (biasanya sejak remaja), muncul dalam banyak aspek kehidupan, dan menyebabkan banyak tekanan atau masalah dalam hubungan dan menghadapi kehidupan.

Kira-kira 1 daripada 20 orang hidup dengan kecelaruan personaliti. Ia bukan kecacatan watak atau sesuatu yang dipilih. Ia biasanya berpunca daripada gabungan faktor genetik dan pengalaman hidup, yang bagi ramai orang termasuk trauma atau pengabaian semasa kecil.

Jenis-jenis

Doktor sering mengelompokkan kecelaruan personaliti kepada tiga kumpulan:

Kumpulan A: pelik atau luar biasaParanoid (sangat curiga terhadap orang lain), skizoid (lebih suka bersendirian), skizotipal (fikiran dan tingkah laku yang luar biasa)
Kumpulan B: emosional dan impulsifAmbang (borderline), antisosial (tidak mempedulikan orang lain dan peraturan), histrionik (sangat perlukan perhatian), narsisistik (sangat perlukan pujian)

Pakar psikiatri membuat diagnosis selepas penilaian yang teliti, selalunya lebih daripada satu lawatan, kerana tanda-tandanya boleh bertindih dengan masalah lain.

  • Terapi bercakap ialah rawatan utama, selalunya selama setahun atau lebih. Contohnya terapi tingkah laku dialektik (DBT) dan terapi berasaskan mentalisasi (MBT).
  • Tiada ubat yang merawat kecelaruan personaliti itu sendiri, tetapi ubat boleh membantu masalah yang datang bersamanya, seperti kemurungan atau kebimbangan.
  • Ramai orang bertambah baik dengan ketara dari semasa ke semasa, terutamanya dengan sokongan.

  • Belajar tentang keadaan anda dan perhatikan pencetusnya.
  • Kekalkan rutin yang tetap, dengan tidur yang teratur.
  • Elakkan alkohol dan dadah.
  • Buat pelan krisis bersama pasukan anda: siapa yang perlu dihubungi dan apa yang membantu.
Borderline personality disorder (BPD)Kecelaruan personaliti ambang (BPD)Intense emotions, relationships and sense of selfEmosi, hubungan dan identiti diri yang sangat kuat dan berubah-ubah

What is it?

BPD is one of the personality disorders most often seen in mental health services. It affects how a person feels and manages emotions, how they see themselves, and how they relate to others. Emotions can be very intense and change quickly. Signs usually start in the teenage years.

Common signs

  • Strong emotions and mood swings that can change within hours
  • A strong fear of being left or abandoned
  • Intense relationships that swing between closeness and conflict
  • An unclear or changing sense of who you are
  • Impulsive actions, such as spending, drinking or risky behaviour
  • Self-harm, or thoughts of suicide
  • Feeling empty inside, or intense anger that is hard to control
  • Feeling suspicious or "unreal" when very stressed

What may contribute to it?

A mix of genes and life experiences. Many people with BPD have been through trauma or neglect in childhood, but not everyone.

How is it treated?

  • Psychological therapy lasting about a year or longer is the main treatment, such as DBT or MBT. These teach skills to manage emotions and relationships.
  • Medicine is not usually used to treat BPD itself. It may help for a short time in a crisis, or for depression or anxiety that come with it.
  • Many people get much better over time. Symptoms often ease with treatment and as people get older.

What can I do to help myself?

  • Name the emotion ("I feel angry", "I feel scared"). Naming it can make it less overwhelming.
  • Ride the wave: strong urges rise, peak and pass. Try to wait before acting.
  • Use calming skills: slow breathing or grounding, a walk, or calling someone you trust.
  • Keep regular sleep and meals, and avoid alcohol and drugs.
  • Make a crisis plan with your team.

For family

  • Stay calm. Acknowledge feelings even if you disagree with the behaviour: "I can see you're really upset."
  • Set clear, kind limits, and keep to them.
  • Take any talk of suicide or self-harm seriously.
  • Look after your own wellbeing too.

When should I seek help?

If you have hurt yourself or are thinking of suicide, get help now: Get Help.

Apakah ia?

BPD ialah antara kecelaruan personaliti yang paling kerap dilihat di perkhidmatan kesihatan mental. Ia menjejaskan cara seseorang merasai dan mengurus emosi, cara dia melihat diri sendiri, dan cara dia berhubung dengan orang lain. Emosi boleh menjadi sangat kuat dan cepat berubah. Tandanya biasanya bermula pada usia remaja.

Tanda-tanda biasa

  • Emosi yang kuat dan perubahan mood yang boleh berlaku dalam beberapa jam
  • Ketakutan yang kuat akan ditinggalkan
  • Hubungan yang sangat rapat tetapi kerap bergolak
  • Rasa tidak pasti atau berubah-ubah tentang siapa diri anda
  • Tindakan impulsif, seperti berbelanja, minum alkohol atau tingkah laku berisiko
  • Mencederakan diri, atau fikiran untuk membunuh diri
  • Rasa kosong dalam diri, atau marah yang kuat dan sukar dikawal
  • Rasa curiga atau "tidak nyata" apabila sangat tertekan

Apa yang mungkin menyumbang?

Gabungan faktor genetik dan pengalaman hidup. Ramai penghidap BPD pernah mengalami trauma atau pengabaian semasa kecil, tetapi bukan semua.

Bagaimana ia dirawat?

  • Terapi psikologi selama kira-kira setahun atau lebih ialah rawatan utama, seperti DBT atau MBT. Terapi ini mengajar kemahiran mengurus emosi dan hubungan.
  • Ubat biasanya tidak digunakan untuk merawat BPD itu sendiri. Ia mungkin membantu untuk tempoh singkat semasa krisis, atau untuk kemurungan atau kebimbangan yang datang bersamanya.
  • Ramai orang bertambah baik dengan ketara dari semasa ke semasa. Simptom selalunya berkurang dengan rawatan dan apabila usia meningkat.

Apa yang boleh saya lakukan untuk membantu diri?

  • Namakan emosi itu ("Saya rasa marah", "Saya rasa takut"). Menamakannya boleh menjadikannya kurang membebankan.
  • Biarkan gelombang berlalu: desakan yang kuat akan naik, memuncak dan reda. Cuba tunggu sebelum bertindak.
  • Gunakan kemahiran menenangkan diri: pernafasan perlahan atau latihan 5-4-3-2-1, berjalan, atau menelefon seseorang yang anda percayai.
  • Kekalkan tidur dan waktu makan yang tetap, dan elakkan alkohol dan dadah.
  • Buat pelan krisis bersama pasukan anda.

Untuk keluarga

  • Bertenang. Akui perasaannya walaupun anda tidak bersetuju dengan tingkah lakunya: "Saya nampak kamu sangat sedih."
  • Tetapkan had yang jelas dengan cara yang baik, dan patuhinya.
  • Ambil serius sebarang percakapan tentang membunuh diri atau mencederakan diri.
  • Jaga kesejahteraan diri anda juga.

How alcohol and drugs affect mental health, and getting help to cut down.Bagaimana alkohol dan dadah menjejaskan kesihatan mental, dan bantuan untuk mengurangkannya.

Alcohol, drugs and mental healthAlkohol, dadah dan kesihatan mentalWhy they make things worse, and how to cut down safelyMengapa ia memburukkan keadaan, dan cara mengurangkannya dengan selamat

How do they affect mental health?

Alcohol and drugs can seem to help for a short while, but they usually make low mood, anxiety and sleep worse. They can also stop your medicine from working properly.

  • Alcohol slows the brain down. Regular drinking can deepen low mood and worry, and makes it more likely that a person acts on thoughts of suicide.
  • Cannabis (ganja), methamphetamine (syabu, ice) and other drugs can trigger psychosis, or bring it back in people who have had it before.
  • Mixing alcohol with some medicines, especially sleeping or calming pills, can cause dangerous drowsiness.

Signs it may be a problem

  • needing a drink or drug to cope, relax or sleep
  • drinking more, or more often, than you meant to
  • family or friends are worried about your drinking or drug use
  • problems at work, at home or with money because of it
  • feeling shaky, sweaty or unwell when you stop

Cutting down

  • Set a goal, and tell someone you trust.
  • Have alcohol-free days. Avoid places and people linked with heavy drinking.
  • Use other ways to cope: slow breathing, a walk, or talking to someone.

If you drink heavily every day, don't stop suddenly on your own. Stopping suddenly can cause shaking, sweating, seeing things, confusion or fits, which can be dangerous. Talk to your doctor first so it can be done safely.

Getting help

Tell your care team at your next visit, or call the Psychiatry unit. You will not be judged. If someone is very confused, having a fit or is unsafe, call 999.

Bagaimana ia menjejaskan kesihatan mental?

  • Alkohol melambatkan fungsi otak. Minum alkohol secara kerap boleh memburukkan mood rendah dan kerisauan, dan menjadikan seseorang lebih mungkin bertindak atas fikiran untuk membunuh diri.
  • Ganja, syabu (methamphetamine, ais) dan dadah lain boleh mencetuskan psikosis, atau menyebabkannya kembali bagi mereka yang pernah mengalaminya.
  • Mencampurkan alkohol dengan sesetengah ubat, terutamanya pil tidur atau penenang, boleh menyebabkan rasa sangat mengantuk yang berbahaya.

Tanda ia mungkin menjadi masalah

  • perlukan alkohol atau dadah untuk menghadapi masalah, bertenang atau tidur
  • minum lebih banyak, atau lebih kerap, daripada yang dirancang

What is relapse?

A relapse is when the symptoms of an illness come back, or get worse, after a period of being well. It can happen with psychosis, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, depression and other conditions. Relapse is not a failure, and it can often be prevented or caught early.

Common causes

  • Stopping or missing medicine
  • Missing clinic appointments
  • Poor or irregular sleep
  • Stress, such as family, money or work problems
  • Alcohol and drugs (such as syabu or ganja)
  • Physical illness

Early warning signs

Relapse usually gives warning signs days or weeks before. Common ones are:

  • sleeping less, or much more
  • feeling tense, irritable or suspicious
  • staying alone, not wanting to see people
  • trouble concentrating
  • not looking after yourself (washing, eating)
  • voices or strange thoughts coming back
  • feeling very low, or unusually high and fast
  • missing medicine, or drinking more alcohol

Each person has their own pattern. Write yours in your Relapse Prevention Plan.

  • Ask a family member to come with you.

Go to the Emergency Department or call 999 now if someone is unsafe, very confused or agitated, not eating or drinking, or talks about suicide or harming others. Hospital Serian's Emergency Department is open 24 hours: call the hospital on 082-874 311 and ask for the Emergency Department.

My medicineUbat sayaTaking it safely, side effects, and when to get helpMengambilnya dengan selamat, kesan sampingan, dan bila perlu bantuan

General information only. Your doctor and pharmacist will advise on your own medicine.

Why medicine helps

Medicine can ease symptoms and help stop them coming back. It works best together with support from your care team and family, and healthy routines.

Taking it

  • Take it the way your doctor or pharmacist told you, at the same time each day. A phone alarm or weekly pill box helps.
  • Missed a dose? Check the label or ask the pharmacy. Never take two doses together to catch up, unless you are told to.
  • Drowsiness: some medicines make you drowsy or slow, especially in the first weeks. Don't drive, ride a motorcycle, or use machines or farm tools such as a chainsaw until you know how the medicine affects you, and never when you feel drowsy.
  • Don't stop your medicine suddenly. This can bring symptoms back, or make you feel unwell (for example dizzy, sick, restless or unable to sleep) because the medicine was stopped too quickly. If you want to reduce or stop it, talk to your care team first.
  • Tell your team about other medicines, herbal or traditional remedies (jamu, ubat kampung, ketum), alcohol, and pregnancy or plans for pregnancy.

Side effects

Many are mild and settle in 1–2 weeks, such as sleepiness, a dry mouth or mild nausea. Tell your care team about anything that bothers you. Feeling so restless inside that you can't sit still: tell your care team soon. It can be treated.

Get help the same day, or go to the Emergency Department, if you have:

  • Very stiff muscles with fever or confusion
  • Sudden stiff, twisted or locked neck, jaw or tongue, eyes rolling upwards, or trouble swallowing, usually soon after starting or increasing a medicine or injection
  • If you take lithium: being sick, diarrhoea, a shake in your hands that is getting worse, feeling unsteady, slurred speech, blurred vision or confusion. These can mean too much lithium. Go to the Emergency Department now and bring all your medicines.
  • A rash with fever, blisters, or sores in the mouth or eyes
  • Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or trouble breathing
  • Fever with a sore throat or mouth ulcers
  • A fit (seizure), fainting, or chest pain
  • New or worse thoughts of suicide, especially soon after starting or changing a medicine: Get Help

These are rare. If in doubt, call or come in. We would rather check.

Safety at home

Keep medicine away from children. If anyone is worried about safety, a trusted family member can keep the medicine and hand out each dose.

Mengapa ubat membantu

Ubat boleh meredakan simptom dan membantu mengelakkannya berulang. Ia paling berkesan bersama sokongan pasukan rawatan dan keluarga, serta rutin yang sihat.

Cara mengambilnya

  • Ambil seperti yang diarahkan oleh doktor atau ahli farmasi, pada waktu yang sama setiap hari. Penggera telefon atau kotak ubat mingguan boleh membantu.
  • Terlupa satu dos? Semak label atau tanya farmasi. Jangan sekali-kali ambil dua dos serentak untuk menggantikannya, kecuali diarahkan.
  • Mengantuk: sesetengah ubat menyebabkan mengantuk atau lambat bertindak, terutama pada minggu-minggu awal. Jangan memandu, menunggang motosikal, atau menggunakan mesin atau alat pertanian seperti gergaji mesin sehingga anda tahu kesan ubat itu, dan jangan sekali-kali ketika mengantuk.
  • Jangan berhenti makan ubat secara mengejut. Ini boleh menyebabkan simptom kembali, atau badan rasa tidak selesa (contohnya pening, loya, susah tidur atau gelisah) kerana ubat dihentikan tiba-tiba. Jika mahu kurangkan atau berhenti, bincang dahulu dengan pasukan rawatan anda.
  • Beritahu pasukan anda tentang ubat lain, ubat herba atau tradisional (jamu, ubat kampung, ketum), alkohol, dan kehamilan atau rancangan untuk hamil.

Kesan sampingan

Kebanyakannya ringan dan reda dalam 1–2 minggu, seperti mengantuk, mulut kering atau loya ringan. Beritahu pasukan rawatan anda tentang apa-apa yang mengganggu. Rasa sangat gelisah dalam diri sehingga tidak boleh duduk diam: beritahu pasukan rawatan anda secepat mungkin. Ia boleh dirawat.

Dapatkan bantuan pada hari yang sama, atau pergi ke Jabatan Kecemasan, jika anda mengalami:

  • Otot sangat keras atau kaku, disertai demam atau kekeliruan
  • Leher, rahang atau lidah tiba-tiba kaku, terpusing atau terkunci, mata terjuling ke atas, atau sukar menelan, biasanya tidak lama selepas memulakan atau menambah ubat atau suntikan
  • Jika anda mengambil litium: muntah, cirit-birit, tangan menggeletar yang semakin teruk, rasa tidak stabil, pertuturan pelat, penglihatan kabur atau keliru. Ini mungkin tanda litium terlalu tinggi. Pergi ke Jabatan Kecemasan segera dan bawa semua ubat anda.
  • Ruam disertai demam, lepuh, atau luka di mulut atau mata
  • Bengkak pada muka, bibir atau tekak, atau sukar bernafas
  • Demam disertai sakit tekak atau ulser mulut
  • Sawan, pengsan, atau sakit dada
  • Fikiran membunuh diri yang baharu atau bertambah teruk, terutamanya selepas memulakan atau menukar ubat: Dapatkan Bantuan

Ini jarang berlaku. Jika ragu-ragu, telefon atau datang. Lebih baik kami periksa.

Keselamatan di rumah

Simpan ubat jauh dari kanak-kanak. Jika ada kebimbangan tentang keselamatan, ahli keluarga yang dipercayai boleh menyimpan ubat dan memberikan setiap dos.

Getting ready for your appointmentBersedia untuk temu janjiWhat to note and bring, so the visit helps you mostApa yang perlu dicatat dan dibawa, supaya lawatan lebih bermanfaat

A little preparation helps you get the most from a short visit. You can also fill in the appointment form below and show it on your phone, or print it.

Before you come, think about

  • how you have felt since your last visit: better, the same or worse
  • new symptoms, or changes in sleep and appetite
  • whether you have taken your medicine as prescribed, and any side effects
  • alcohol, smoking or other substances
  • big events: work, family, money or health
  • your top 3 questions for the doctor

Bring with you

  • your appointment book
  • all your medicines, in their packets
  • your MyKad
  • a family member, if that helps you

If you have thoughts of harming yourself, tell staff as soon as you arrive. If you feel unsafe now, don't wait for your appointment: Get Help.

Sedikit persediaan membantu anda mendapat manfaat penuh daripada lawatan yang singkat. Anda juga boleh mengisi borang temu janji di bawah dan menunjukkannya di telefon anda, atau mencetaknya.

Sebelum datang, fikirkan tentang

  • perasaan anda sejak lawatan lepas: lebih baik, sama atau lebih teruk
  • simptom baharu, atau perubahan pada tidur dan selera makan
  • sama ada anda mengambil ubat seperti yang diarahkan, dan sebarang kesan sampingan
  • alkohol, merokok atau bahan lain
  • peristiwa besar: kerja, keluarga, wang atau kesihatan
  • 3 soalan utama anda untuk doktor

Bawa bersama

  • buku temu janji anda
  • semua ubat anda, dalam paketnya
  • MyKad anda
  • ahli keluarga, jika itu membantu anda
